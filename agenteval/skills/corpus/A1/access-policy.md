# Northwind Ltd — Access Control Policy

Version 3.2, approved by the IT Steering Group on 14 February 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind grants, reviews and removes access to its
information systems. It applies to employees, contractors and temporary staff.

## 2. Passwords

2.1 Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and must include a number.
2.2 Passwords expire every 90 days and the last five passwords cannot be reused.
2.3 Passwords must never be shared, written down or sent by email.

## 3. Remote access

3.1 Staff may work remotely using a company laptop.
3.2 Remote access through the VPN requires a username and password.
3.3 Public Wi-Fi may be used only while the VPN is connected.

## 4. Granting and reviewing access

4.1 Line managers request access through the service desk.
4.2 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege.
4.3 User access rights are reviewed once a year by each system owner.
4.4 Review results are kept by the IT Security team.

## 5. Leavers

5.1 HR notifies the service desk of every leaver at least five days in advance.
5.2 All access for a leaver is removed no later than the end of their last working day.

## 6. Privileged access

6.1 Administrators use a dedicated admin account that is separate from the account they use for everyday work.
6.2 Admin accounts may not be used to read email or browse the internet.
