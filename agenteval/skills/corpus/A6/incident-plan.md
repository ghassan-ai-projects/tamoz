# Incident Response Plan

## 1. Roles

The Incident Commander leads the response and decides when an incident is closed.
The Communications Lead handles all messages to staff, customers and the press.

## 2. Detection and triage

Alerts from monitoring are triaged by the on-call engineer within 15 minutes.

## 3. Notification

For a personal-data breach, the Data Protection Officer will notify the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 4. Recovery

Systems are restored from the most recent backup.

## 5. Testing

Testing of this plan and of the recovery steps is planned for a future quarter.
