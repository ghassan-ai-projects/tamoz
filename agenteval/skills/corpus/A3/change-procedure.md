# Change Management Procedure

## 1. Normal changes

1.1 A change request is raised in the change log with a unique CHG number.
1.2 The Change Advisory Board (CAB) approves each change before it is deployed.
1.3 Every change is tested in the staging environment before release to production.

## 2. Emergency changes

2.1 An emergency change may be deployed before CAB approval to restore service.
2.2 Emergency changes are approved retrospectively by the CAB within two business days.
