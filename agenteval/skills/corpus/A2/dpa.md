# Data Processing Agreement

Between Northwind Ltd ("Customer") and CloudLedger GmbH ("Processor").

## 1. Scope

1.1 The Processor processes Customer Data only to provide the accounting service
described in the Master Services Agreement.
1.2 The Processor processes Customer Data only on documented instructions from the Customer.

## 2. Security

2.1 The Processor implements the technical and organisational measures listed in Annex B.
2.2 The Processor shall notify the Customer of any Personal Data Breach within ten (10) business days of becoming aware of it.
2.3 Notification shall describe the nature of the breach and the measures taken.

## 3. Sub-processors

3.1 The Processor shall not engage any sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.
3.2 The Processor remains liable for the performance of any authorised sub-processor.

## 4. Term and termination

4.1 This Agreement ends when the Master Services Agreement ends.
4.2 On termination the Processor may retain Customer Data for as long as necessary for its business purposes.

## 5. Audit

5.1 The Customer may audit the Processor's compliance with this Agreement once per calendar year on thirty days' notice.
