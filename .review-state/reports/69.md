- **C1 (apply-intent): deduplicate the numeric-spelling test in `classify_address`.**
  Lines 108–110 re-enumerate inline exactly the three alternatives that
  `NUMERIC_IP_PATTERN` (line 38) already names ("bare decimal / hex / octal spellings of
  an IPv4 address") and that `ip_literal?` uses. Replace the disjunction with
  `NUMERIC_IP_PATTERN.match?(text)`.
  Proof of behavioral identity: `\A(?:\d+|0[xX][0-9A-Fa-f]+|0[0-7]+)\z` with alternation
  inside the anchored group is equivalent to `\A\d+\z || \A0[xX]…\z || \A0[0-7]+\z`
  (each alternative is independently fully anchored); in Ruby `\d` == `[0-9]`. Both
  forms answer true iff one alternative matches wholly. Downstream selection of
  decimal/hex/octal stays in `normalize_numeric_ipv4` (untouched). Guarded by
  `WebsearchAdapterTest#test_exotic_literals_are_neutralized_before_classification`
  ("2130706433", "0x7f000001", "017700000001", "0xC0A80101", IPv4-mapped forms) and
  `#test_unclassifiable_ip_spellings_are_refused_fail_closed`.
