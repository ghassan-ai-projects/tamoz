# Code of Conduct

Tamoz is an open project, and this code of conduct is how we keep it that way.
It applies to every space where the project is discussed — issue trackers, pull
requests, chats, and maintainer review.

## Our Standard

- Critique ideas without attacking people. A design can be wrong; a person is not.
- Keep feedback actionable. Say what should change and why, not just that something is bad.
- Assume good faith. Ask for the intent behind a change before assuming the worst.
- Respect differing experience levels. Explain the reasoning, not just the rule.
- Own mistakes and fix them. Acknowledging an error early beats defending it late.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment, deliberate intimidation, or stalking in any form.
- Publishing private information — credentials, prompts, or traces — without consent.
- Dismissing a contribution because of who made it rather than what it contains.
- Repeated unconstructive disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers respond to reported violations and may remove comments or close
discussions that breach this policy. Serious or repeated violations can lead to
a temporary or permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers — through the repository's security
advisory channel or a direct message — never in a public issue.
